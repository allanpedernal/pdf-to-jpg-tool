================================================================
SEO & FUNNEL PLAN — pdf-to-jpg-tool.com
Goal: rank in Google for "pdf to jpg" and related searches
Date: 12 June 2026
================================================================

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. REALITY CHECK (read this first)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- "pdf to jpg" gets roughly 400-600k global searches/month and is
  dominated by Adobe, iLovePDF, Smallpdf and Canva (DA 90+ sites).
  A newer site cannot win that head term in months. The path is:
  (1) own the long-tail variants first, (2) build real backlinks,
  (3) let the head term follow in 9-18 months.
- Paid search is economically upside-down for this site: a click
  for "pdf to jpg" costs ~$0.30-$1.50, while AdSense earns roughly
  $2-8 per 1,000 pageviews (~$0.005 per view). Buying traffic to
  an ad-monetised free tool loses money. Spend effort, not budget.
- Your one real differentiator vs every big competitor: files are
  processed in the browser, never uploaded. Lead with privacy in
  every title, description and pitch.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE FUNNEL
----------------------------------------------------------------
TOFU (awareness)
  Searches: "why is my jpg blurry", "what dpi for printing"
  Asset:    blog posts (your Mon/Wed/Fri auto-blog pipeline)
  Next:     inline links to tool pages

MOFU (evaluation)
  Searches: "best free pdf to jpg converter", "pdf to jpg
            without upload", "is it safe to use online converters"
  Asset:    comparison/listicle posts + privacy-angle articles
  Next:     tool page

BOFU (action)
  Searches: "pdf to jpg", "convert pdf to jpg free"
  Asset:    pdf-to-jpg.html and the sibling tool pages
  Next:     use the tool -> AdSense impressions

RETENTION
  Asset:    PWA install prompt, related-tool cross-links
  Value:    repeat/direct traffic, branded searches (a ranking
            signal in itself)

Because revenue is AdSense RPM, the funnel goal is sessions x
pages-per-session. Internal linking matters as much as rankings.

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. PHASE 1 — FOUNDATION (week 1)
----------------------------------------------------------------
a) Google Search Console: verify the domain property and submit
   sitemap.xml. You have IndexNow but no GSC verification found.
   Without GSC you have zero keyword/coverage data. Do this first.
b) Regenerate tool pages: the tool pages on disk are STALE — they
   are missing the unique SEO content sections that
   scripts/build-tool-pages.js is designed to inject. As deployed,
   your money pages have almost no crawlable text (the tool UI is
   injected by JavaScript). Run: node scripts/build-tool-pages.js
   and verify each page then carries its H1/intro/how-to/FAQ.
c) Tool -> blog internal links: blog posts already link to tools,
   but tool pages never link back. Add a "related guides" block
   (2-3 contextual blog links) to the tool page template.
d) Related-tools block on every tool page ("Also need to:
   Compress PDF / PDF to PNG / JPG to PDF") — raises pages per
   session and spreads link equity.
e) Visible breadcrumbs on tool pages (BreadcrumbList schema
   already exists in places — render it for users too).
f) Unique og:image per tool page (all pages currently share
   preview.jpg). 1200x630, tool name + "free, no upload".
g) Sitemap lastmod: stamp real per-file dates (e.g. from git
   history) instead of "today" for everything — Google learns to
   distrust always-fresh lastmod values.
h) Fix the topic queue data: scripts/topics.json relatedTools all
   point to "/" — point them at the real tool pages
   (/pdf-to-jpg.html etc.). Same fix in the generate-post.js
   prompt, which currently tells the AI to use href="/".

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. PHASE 2 — KEYWORD-MAPPED CONTENT ENGINE (weeks 1-8)
----------------------------------------------------------------
The auto-blog (generate-post.js + topics.json, Mon/Wed/Fri) is the
engine. The work is feeding it the right keyword map. Reorder /
extend topics.json with these clusters, highest priority first:

PLATFORM LONG-TAILS (low competition, direct intent — do first)
  - How to convert PDF to JPG on iPhone (free, no app)
  - ... on Mac — 3 free ways
  - ... on Windows 11 without installing software
  - ... on Android (free, no app)
  - ... on a Chromebook

PRIVACY ANGLE (your differentiator — no big competitor can claim it)
  - How to convert PDF to JPG without uploading your file anywhere
  - Is it safe to use online PDF converters? What to check first

QUALITY / SPEC LONG-TAILS
  - PDF to JPG in high resolution (300 DPI for print)
  - Convert a multi-page PDF into separate JPG images (batch + ZIP)
  - PDF to JPG without losing quality (already published — good)

MOFU LISTICLES (these attract backlinks)
  - "7 best free PDF to JPG converters compared (2026)" — include
    competitors honestly, win on the no-upload/privacy axis.

SIBLING-TOOL CLUSTERS (rank faster, lift overall domain authority)
  - Mirror the same clusters for HEIC-to-JPG and the WebP tools —
    far lower competition than pdf-to-jpg, quicker page-1 wins.

Keep the human PR-review step in the workflow. It is the guardrail
against a repeat of the AdSense "low-value content" rejection and
against Google's scaled-content policy.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. PHASE 3 — AUTHORITY & BACKLINKS (weeks 2-12, ongoing)
----------------------------------------------------------------
This is the bottleneck for the head term. In order of effort/return:
a) Free-tool directories: AlternativeTo, Product Hunt, SaaSHub,
   Slant, ToolFinder, free-for.dev-style lists. 10-20 decent links,
   roughly an afternoon each.
b) Open-source angle: if the repo can be public, a proper GitHub
   README with the live link earns organic stars/links; submit to
   "awesome-pdf" style lists.
c) Privacy story pitch: short outreach to privacy/productivity
   newsletters and relevant subreddits — "a PDF converter that
   never uploads your file" is genuinely pitchable. Post as a
   maker, not as spam.
d) Linkable asset: publish a "DPI / image-size cheat sheet"
   reference page (pixel dimensions for A4/Letter/photo sizes at
   150/300/600 DPI). Bloggers cite pages like this; it earns links
   passively and links out to your resize/convert tools.

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. PHASE 4 — PAID (small and surgical, optional)
----------------------------------------------------------------
- SKIP Google Ads on "pdf to jpg" (negative ROI vs AdSense — see
  section 1).
- If you want to spend something: Microsoft/Bing Ads, exact-match
  long-tails only, $5-10/day cap. CPCs are often $0.05-0.20. Use it
  to learn which queries produce multi-page sessions, then kill it
  after 4-6 weeks of data.
- Product Hunt launch day is the highest-ROI "campaign" available
  and costs nothing: backlinks + traffic spike + branded searches.

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. PHASE 5 — INTERNATIONAL (months 3-6, biggest untapped lever)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Conversion-tool traffic is majority non-English and far less
competitive. Translate the top 5 tool pages (Spanish, Portuguese,
Indonesian, Hindi, French) as static pages with proper hreflang.
Defer until Phases 1-3 show traction.

8. KPIs & TIMELINE
----------------------------------------------------------------
0-3 months   All pages indexed in GSC. Long-tails (platform
             queries, "heic to jpg no upload") reach page 1-2.
             1-5k organic visits/month.
3-6 months   Mid-tails ("convert pdf to jpg high resolution")
             top 10. 10-30k visits/month. 20+ referring domains.
6-12 months  Secondary head terms ("pdf to jpg converter free no
             upload") page 1. "pdf to jpg" top 20.
12-18 months "pdf to jpg" page-1 contention — only if the backlink
             work in Phase 3 actually happened.

Track: GSC (queries/positions), GA4 (pdf_conversion event is
already wired + pages/session), and a free rank tracker for ~20
mapped keywords.

9. MONETIZATION BEYOND ADSENSE (GitHub Pages compatible)
----------------------------------------------------------------
Constraint: static hosting only — no backend, no accounts, no
metered APIs. Everything below works on GitHub Pages as-is.
Ranked by fit with the current setup:

a) AFFILIATE LINKS (best fit — start now)
   Slots straight into the blog/comparison content engine.
   - PDF software: Wondershare PDFelement, UPDF, Soda PDF run
     affiliate programs, often 30-50% on a $50-80 license. Frame
     as "when you need more than a free tool" inside posts.
   - VPNs / privacy tools: NordVPN, Surfshark, Proton pay $30-100+
     per signup. Natural match for the "no upload, privacy-first"
     positioning and the "is it safe to use online converters?"
     article cluster.
   - Amazon Associates: scanners, printers, photo paper inside
     DPI/printing/scanning articles. Low rates (~3%), zero
     friction to join.
   Expectation: starts at tens of $/month at current traffic, but
   scales with content quality — one well-ranked comparison post
   can out-earn the whole site's display ads.

b) DONATIONS (one hour of work, small but free money)
   Buy Me a Coffee / Ko-fi / GitHub Sponsors button in the footer
   plus a soft ask after a successful conversion ("This tool is
   free and keeps your files private — buy us a coffee").
   Privacy-conscious users are the demographic most likely to tip.

c) "PRO" UNLOCK VIA GUMROAD / LEMON SQUEEZY (no backend needed)
   They handle checkout, payment and license keys on their side;
   your static JS validates the key against their public API and
   unlocks features client-side. Candidate Pro features: bigger
   batch limits, extra OCR languages, no ads, an offline desktop
   build (the PWA wrapped in Electron, sold as a download).
   Client-side gating is piratable — irrelevant in practice;
   honest users pay, and a $10-15 lifetime unlock from 0.1% of
   users beats their AdSense value many times over.

d) BETTER AD NETWORKS LATER (traffic-gated — know the thresholds)
   - Ezoic: works with GitHub Pages via DNS integration; pointless
     under ~10k visits/month.
   - Monumetric: 10k pageviews/month minimum.
   - Mediavine: 50k sessions/month minimum.
   These typically pay 2-5x AdSense RPM. Until then, keep AdSense.

e) WHITE-LABEL / SELL THE CODE
   A "self-host this tool suite on your own domain" license on
   Gumroad, marketed through a public GitHub repo. Agencies and
   niche-site builders pay $50-200 for this. Small revenue, and it
   doubles as the Phase-3 backlink play.

SKIP: sponsorship slots and newsletter monetization (need traffic
you don't have yet), crypto ad networks (poison AdSense standing),
anything needing a backend (paid API, accounts, usage-limited
subscriptions) — that's a different architecture (e.g. Cloudflare
Workers), not GitHub Pages.

Order of execution: affiliate links into existing/planned content
-> donation button -> Pro unlock. All three coexist with AdSense.
None of them creates traffic — they multiply it. SEO comes first.
